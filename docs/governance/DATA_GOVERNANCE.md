# Data governance decisions

Before processing real personal or health data, establish:
- Controller, joint-controller and processor roles for each purpose and data flow.
- Applicable lawful basis and any required consent or patient information.
- DPO and registration obligations under Rwanda's data-protection framework.
- Data classification, minimum necessary fields, purpose limitation and retention schedule.
- Data-subject rights handling, breach response and audit responsibilities.
- Approved hosting locations, subprocessors and any cross-border transfer assessment/authorization.
- Institutional agreements, access responsibilities and clinical safety escalation.

Consent is not assumed to be the sole lawful basis for every healthcare processing activity. Obtain qualified Rwanda legal/privacy review and approval from the accountable institutions.
